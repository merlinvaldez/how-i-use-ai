# Intro Presentation

## Research the meeting

Use all three connected plugins. Load their relevant skills when available, and use the plugin's search and read tools if a matching skill is unavailable.

1. **Gmail.** Search Work Gmail for the organization, contact, opportunity name, and solicitation ID. Read the matching threads for the meeting purpose, referrals, requested materials, stated needs, and commitments. Verify recipients before drafting any follow-up.
2. **Notion.** Use Notion Meeting Intelligence and Research Documentation to find the Sales CRM opportunity and related meeting notes. Read the record, page body, and linked materials. Capture the audience, procurement route, stage, and any next action already agreed.
3. **Google Drive.** Use Google Drive and Google Slides to locate the approved master, prior decks, capability statement, approved visuals, and opportunity files. Use Presentations for PowerPoint authoring and visual review.

Resolve discrepancies using the latest direct evidence. Distinguish confirmed agency work and procurement routes from potential scopes to discuss.

## Choose the audience and build the deck

- **Procurement or supplier diversity office.** Identify what the agency buys, who owns the program need, and which purchasing routes are relevant. Put audience-specific support early in the deck. End with a concrete ask, such as a program-team introduction or guidance on being considered for small purchases.
- **Prime contractor.** Research the prime's agency work and relevant bids. Identify a specific workstream the user’s team could own, the deliverables it could take responsibility for, and how it would work with the prime. End with a next step such as reviewing a scope, identifying a delivery owner, or exchanging capability statements.

Build a short meeting deck. Introduce the senior team, select relevant capabilities, show evidence of delivery, explain the approach, and close with a specific ask. Adapt the title and opportunity slides to the meeting. Verify certifications, client relationships, past-performance claims, and outcomes before including them.

## Use the approved style and visual examples

Duplicate the approved master or the relevant derivative. Preserve the master’s typography, spacing, logo proportions, circular portraits, service and evidence layouts, icons, and process timeline. Keep the editable PowerPoint when the native on-click reveals matter.

- Ask for the approved master, reuse guidance, and any examples the user wants to match.
- Use approved portraits, client logos, and product screenshots where relevant and cleared for reuse.

## Verify, file, and follow up

1. Render the deck and inspect each slide for legibility, overflow, image crops, and consistency. Verify that any follow-up PDF shows the intended content from animated slides.
2. In Drive, locate **Proposals → [year] → [Opportunity Name]**. Reuse the matching folder or create it if missing. Use the organization name for an introductory meeting without a named opportunity.
3. Save the editable deck in that folder and place the PDF alongside it when preparing a follow-up. Verify both files are in the intended folder and return direct links.
4. When requested, draft the thank-you in the existing Gmail thread with the PDF and any agreed materials. Send only with explicit authorization.
5. When requested, update the matching Notion Sales CRM record with the deck link, agreed next action, and due date. Verify the update.
